## Short description (max 80 chars)

## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Amardip Elevators is the customer app for Amardip Elevators' maintenance
customers. Use it to:

- View your elevator's upcoming and completed service visits
- Raise a complaint or service request in seconds
- Track a ticket's status from raised to resolved
- See the technician assigned to your job and call them directly
- View the completed job report, including parts used, technician notes,
  and your representative's signature
- Get notified the moment a technician is assigned or your ticket is updated
- Access your AMC/warranty documents

Built for existing Amardip Elevators maintenance contract customers.
